01 / The dough

It starts with dough.

Soft, airy and stretched out to the edge. Every pie begins here, whether it's a 12", 14", 18" or a deep pan.

02 / The sauce

Renzo's signature sauce.

Ladled on in one slow spiral, from the center out toward the crust.

03 / The cheese

Fresh mozzarella.

Torn and scattered generously, the creamy kind that melts into golden pools.

04 / The pepperoni

Then the pepperoni.

Edge to edge, ready to crisp up and cup in the heat.

05 / The basil

Fresh basil.

A handful of leaves for that bright, peppery lift.

06 / The oven

Into the heat.

Until the crust blisters, the cheese bubbles and the edges turn golden.

07 / The finish

A drizzle of hot honey.

Sweet heat to finish it off.

08 / Ready

The Spicy Renzo.

Fresh mozzarella, pepperoni, basil and hot honey.

Our story

Cooked from our hearts.

Italian food is about passion, and you can taste it in every bite of our traditional cooking. Nothing too trendy, just the simple pleasures: familiar, flavorful sauces, tender pasta, succulent meats, and gourmet and gluten-free pizzas, all made to order.

We've been feeding South Florida since 1989 and East Boca for more than twenty years, and we still treat every table like family.
